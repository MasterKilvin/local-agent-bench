# Q8 showed no advantage over Q4 at matched 24K context and took 2.3 times as long per attempt; plus exploratory thinking-level and KV-cache runs

*Report 3 of 5. Method and scoring: report 1. Also covers the exploratory engine settings: KV-cache quantization,
thinking level and context actually used.*

## The matched comparison
Same model (Qwen3.8-27B), same agent (Pi 0.87.0), same prompts, same 24,576-token context, f16 KV cache, three repeats of
all twenty tasks. Only the weight quantization changed. 24K was chosen because Q8_0 at 32K did not fit: 7% of layers
spilled to CPU.

| | Q4_K_M (arm 9) | Q8_0 (arm 8) |
|---|---|---|
| Solvable passed | 44 of 48 | 42 of 48 |
| Impossible, strict → adjudicated | 6 → 12 of 12 | 6 → 12 of 12 |
| All attempts, strict / adjudicated | 50 / 56 of 60 | 48 / 54 of 60 |
| Median seconds per attempt (failures included) | 18.1 | 42.0 |
| Total agent time, 60 attempts | 23 min | 53 min |
| Median prompt tokens per attempt | 18,400 | 18,335 |
| Peak prompt tokens | 17,151 | 11,815 |
| GPU memory in use, total | about 22 GB | about 29 GB |

Sources: `results/v2/9-pi-27b-q4-24k/`, `results/v2/8-pi-27b-q8-24k/` (each has `results.jsonl`, `relay-calls.jsonl`,
`MANIFEST.txt` with the model digest). GPU totals were read from the driver and do not separate weights from KV cache.

## Where they differed
Tasks with a different number of strict passes, out of 3:

| Task | Q4 | Q8 |
|---|---|---|
| build: JSONL report | 3 | 2 |
| data: inclusive ranges | 3 | 2 (one attempt changed nothing) |
| impossible: retention policy (strict wording) | 3 | 2 |
| impossible: lossless shortening (strict wording) | 1 | 2 |

Both failed the CSV dry-run task on all three attempts (report 1). Two solvable attempts separate the arms. That is not
evidence that Q4 is better, and it is not evidence that the two are equal.

## What this supports
On tasks of this size, at this context, Q8 gave no observed benefit for 2.3 times the time and about 7 GB more memory. Q4
is a reasonable starting point. It does not establish equal quality, and it says nothing about Q8 at 64K, about other
quantization formats such as NVFP4, or about other models.

## Exploratory settings (two repeats, not matched to the three-repeat baseline)
**KV cache q4_0 (arm 5)**, Q4 weights, 24K: 29 of 32 solvable, refusals 5 → 6 of 8, median 18.8 s. Against the f16 control
(arm 9: 44 of 48 solvable; refusals 6 → 12 of 12), this does not establish that cache quantization preserved quality. Peak prompts reached only
13,945 tokens, so long-context behaviour is untested. The engine log confirming the quantized cache is
`results/v2/5-pi-27b-q4-24k-kvq4/engine-log-kv.txt`.

**Thinking level**, Pi + Q4 at 64K:

| | low (arm 6, 2 repeats) | medium (arm 1, 3 repeats) | high (arm 7, 2 repeats) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solvable passed | 28 of 32 | 42 of 48 | 31 of 32 |
| Impossible, strict → adjudicated | 2 → 4 of 8 | 7 → 12 of 12 | 5 → 8 of 8 |
| Reasoning characters per attempt | 14,969 | 5,937 | 18,150 |
| Median seconds per attempt | 36.3 | 17.5 | 44.5 |
| Peak prompt tokens | 18,337 | 19,244 | 42,503 |

Reasoning characters count only calls inside scored attempts; four low-thinking calls fell outside every attempt
and are excluded. On this engine, "low" did not produce less reasoning text than "medium". The setting reached the engine: the relay log
records `reasoning_effort` on every call. Reasoning is measured in characters, not tokens. No optimal setting is
established.

**Context actually used:** 4–6-file projects pushed peak prompts to 19,244 tokens at medium thinking and 42,503 at high.
Small projects do exceed a 16K window.
